

# Hi, I'm Diane Mueller 👋

I'm an open-source community architect with over 30 years of experience helping projects grow healthy, engaged contributor communities and develop sustainable open-source governance.

## 🔭 What I'm Working On

- **[Hedera Hashgraph, LLC](https://hedera.com/)** — Strategic Advisor on Open Source Development, working with the organization responsible for governance of the Hedera public network. My work includes open-source strategy and supporting the growth of [Hiero](https://github.com/hiero-ledger) as an independent, community-governed open-source project.

- **[Linux Foundation Decentralized Trust (LFDT)](https://www.lfdecentralizedtrust.org/)** — Elected member of the Technical Advisory Council (TAC) and LFDT Labs steward. I work with projects on governance, community development, contributor engagement, and long-term sustainability.

- **[Hiero](https://github.com/hiero-ledger)** — Active contributor to community development and governance, supporting contributor and maintainer growth and making it easier for organizations to independently deploy, operate, and build on Hiero networks.

  I'm also a **maintainer of the Hiero DID SDK project**, supporting decentralized identity and interoperability through the development of tools for decentralized identifiers (DIDs) and verifiable credentials:
  - [Hiero DID SDK JavaScript](https://github.com/hiero-ledger/hiero-did-sdk-js)
  - [Hiero DID SDK Python](https://github.com/hiero-ledger/hiero-did-sdk-python)

- **[CLPR (Cross-Ledger Protocol)](https://github.com/LFDT-CLPR/clpr-spec)** — Helped bring CLPR into LFDT Labs, supporting its development as an open-source initiative for interoperability between independent distributed ledger networks. I'm interested in fostering collaboration around open standards and cross-ledger communication across the LFDT ecosystem.

- **[Bitergia](https://bitergia.com/)** — Co-founder and Director of Research and Advisory Services, applying open-source community analytics to contributor growth, project health, and sustainability.
